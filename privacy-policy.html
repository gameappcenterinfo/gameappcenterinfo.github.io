Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Memory Card Challenge, developed by Gaming App Center.

Privacy contact: game.app.center.info@gmail.com

Overview

Memory Card Challenge is a memory card game. You do not need to create an account with Gaming App Center to play. The game does not ask you to provide a name, email address, or phone number for gameplay, and does not request access to your contacts or precise device location. Advertising and analytics services can process device identifiers, approximate location, and other information described below. Optional Google Play Games features use your Google gaming profile when available and enabled.

Data Stored on the Device

The app may store gameplay progress, unlocked levels, completed levels, resume stage, selected language, theme, sound settings, hint preferences, reminder settings, local achievement records, and premium entitlement status on your device. This information provides game functionality and restores your settings and progress. Advertising SDKs may also store downloaded ad content, configuration, and temporary files locally.

Advertising

The app uses Unity Ads for banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Unity Ads may collect and share device and app identifiers, approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and purchase-related information as described in Unity's privacy disclosures. This information is used for advertising, measurement and analytics, service functionality, and fraud prevention. Depending on the privacy choices provided to Unity and the applicable settings, ads may be contextual or personalized.

Some app versions also use Google AdMob. AdMob is currently on hold in the latest app configuration; older distributed versions may continue to use it. Where active, AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver and measure advertising. The presence and behavior of advertising providers can therefore differ between installed versions.

Depending on the app version, ad requests and downloads may start during startup or gameplay, before an ad becomes visible. This preloading keeps an ad ready for a later eligible screen. Downloaded ads remain under the advertising SDK's control and can expire. Loading an ad can involve communication with the provider even when the ad is not displayed. Local house banners promote game features without requesting a paid ad.

Free players may choose rewarded ads for features such as an additional hint or a second chance. Normal retry options remain available when a second-chance ad is declined or unavailable. Premium removes normal gameplay advertisements; analytics and purchase processing are handled separately.

Advertising Privacy Choices

Unity provides privacy information and available choices through the Data Privacy icon in its ads and the rights and contact information in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Unity's available consent prompts and controls depend on the SDK, configuration, and applicable privacy requirements. These controls apply to Unity advertising and do not automatically change Google settings.

In versions where AdMob is active, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform where required before requesting AdMob ads. When Google requires a persistent privacy entry point, Privacy Choices is available under Options > More Options. This Google control is separate from Unity's controls. Device settings may also provide advertising identifier and ad-personalization controls.

Analytics

Android builds use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand game activity, such as progress through levels and stages, stage performance, app interactions, app settings, ad interaction outcomes, and purchase flow outcomes. Google Analytics for Firebase may also process app-instance identifiers, advertising identifiers, device information, and similar technical data according to Google's policies. This information helps us improve level balance, difficulty, retention, monetization flow, and app quality. We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, payment card details, or raw device identifiers through our custom gameplay analytics event parameters. In versions where AdMob revenue measurement is active, the app may send AdMob's estimated impression-level ad value, currency, precision, ad format, placement, ad-unit identifier, and mediation-adapter name to Firebase for aggregate advertising-performance measurement.

Google Play Games

Where Google Play Games features are available and enabled, Google may process your gaming-profile identifiers, achievement progress, and leaderboard scores to provide those features. Google manages the gaming profile and related privacy controls. Local game progress is separate from records maintained by Google Play Games.

Optional Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app stores reminder preferences and schedules notifications on your device. You can disable reminders in the app or manage notification permission in Android settings. This notification feature does not require you to provide contact details.

Purchases

The app uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases, including premium access. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play. We do not collect or store payment card details.

Data Sharing

Data may be processed by Unity Ads and its advertising or service partners, Google AdMob where active, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Play Billing, and Google Play Games or other Google Play services. Providers may share information with their service and advertising partners as described in their policies, subject to applicable choices and requirements. These services may process information outside your country; their policies explain their international processing and safeguards.

Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information according to their policies:

  • Unity Ads: Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and advertising information
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: privacy and security
  • Google Play Billing, Google Play Games, and Google Play services: Google Privacy Policy

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect data handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Unity and Google describe their safeguards, including protection of data in transit, in the policies linked above.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game progress remains until you use Erase Local Progress, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Erasing progress within the game does not necessarily remove settings, SDK files, or data held by service providers. Android's app-storage controls can remove app-local settings and cached files. Google Play may restore a valid purchase entitlement when you use purchase restoration.

Unity and Google retain data according to their privacy policies, service settings, and applicable requirements. Firebase Analytics retention also depends on the Analytics property's configured retention settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not automatically delete provider-held advertising, analytics, purchase, or gaming-profile records. Use the providers' privacy controls and request procedures for those records. We do not promise a single retention period for every provider or data type.

Overview

Memory Card Challenge is a memory card game. The app does not require users to create an account, and we do not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location information.

The app may store game progress, preferences, selected language, sound settings, and similar gameplay settings locally on the user's device. This data is used only to provide app functionality and improve the user experience inside the game.

The app uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. Google AdMob may collect device information, advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and related data as described in Google's policies. This data may be used to provide, measure, and improve ads.

The app uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play. We do not collect or store payment card details.

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by third-party services used in the app, including Google AdMob and Google Play Billing, according to their own privacy policies and terms.
